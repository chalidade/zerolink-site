FAQ

Questions people often ask.

Really no internet or mobile credit?

Really. The phone does not even need a SIM card. Only Wi‑Fi and Location have to be on. Internet is only used to download the APK and, if you want, the map of your area.

How far does it reach?

About 50–100 m in the open, less indoors. For more: a bridge phone in the middle, base-camp Wi‑Fi with a long-range antenna (1–3 km), or a Meshtastic LoRa device for messages and SOS between groups up to several km.

How do I update?

Download the latest APK and install it over the old version. Your name, photo and settings are kept.

Can I use it from a laptop, Mac or browser?

Yes. A Windows, Mac or Linux laptop, an iPhone or a tablet joins the group in a browser with no internet, either through an Android phone that opens its group to browsers or through the portable laptop app. See the guide and downloads.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's built, but can't be installed yet. We are still setting up the Apple Developer account, then opening a TestFlight beta. On iPhone, groups connect through Wi‑Fi post mode: one phone creates the group, the others join with a 6‑digit code on the same Wi‑Fi or hotspot. Sign up to hear first.

When will it be on Play Store?

Soon. Until then, install the APK from this page. Once the Play Store version is out you can switch without losing your name and settings. Get told at launch.

My phone can't find the group. Why?

Make sure Wi‑Fi and Location are on on both phones, the Location / Nearby devices permission is granted, and they are within 50–100 m. Some phones need the screen on while searching. If it still fails, swap roles: let the phone that was searching create the group.

Is it free?

Free for everyone, forever, with no ads. For search-and-rescue teams, relief posts or institutions that need training and customisation, get in touch.
